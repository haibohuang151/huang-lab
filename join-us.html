Who we are

We are a collaborative, ambitious, multi-disciplinary research team working at the forefront of food science and technology. We value team play, creativity, hard work, and ethical conduct of research. We welcome students from different disciplines, including food science, agricultural engineering, environmental engineering, chemistry, and biochemistry. We hope you will consider spending time with us.

Current openings

We are recruiting 1–2 Ph.D. or M.S. students for Fall, 2027. Students with a background in Food Science, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Agricultural Engineering, Biological Engineering, Bioprocess Engineering, and other related fields are welcome to apply.

Interested in applying?

Please reach out to Dr. Haibo Huang, Department of Food Science and Technology, Virginia Tech, with your CV and a short note about your research interests.
