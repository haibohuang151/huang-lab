Join Us

We are a collaborative and multidisciplinary research group developing engineering solutions for a more sustainable food and bioprocessing system.

We value curiosity, rigorous thinking, creativity, teamwork, independence, and research integrity. Our members come from diverse backgrounds, including food science, agricultural and biological engineering, chemical engineering, chemistry, biochemistry, and related fields.

We welcome students who are interested not only in conducting high-quality research, but also in learning how scientific discoveries can be translated into practical technologies.

Current Openings

We are recruiting 1–2 Ph.D. or M.S. students for Fall 2027.

Students with backgrounds in food science, chemical engineering, agricultural engineering, biological engineering, bioprocess engineering, chemistry, biochemistry, and related disciplines are encouraged to apply.

What You Can Work On

Research opportunities in the Huang Lab may include:

  • Sustainable processing of food and agricultural byproducts
  • Protein and dietary fiber ingredient development
  • Functional materials derived from agricultural biomass
  • Integrated bioprocess and product-recovery technologies
  • Process modeling, scale-up, techno-economic analysis

Projects frequently cross disciplinary boundaries and provide opportunities to work with collaborators in food science, engineering, chemistry, microbiology, materials science, and industry.

What You Will Learn

Depending on the project, students may gain experience in:

  • Experimental design and quantitative data analysis
  • Food and biomass processing
  • Separation and product-recovery technologies
  • Chemical and structural characterization
  • Process design and scale-up
  • Techno-economic analysis and life cycle assessment

We expect students to develop both deep expertise in their research topic and the ability to think about technology from a broader process and application perspective.

Where Our Trainees Go

Our former trainees have continued their careers as faculty members, national-laboratory researchers, postdoctoral researchers, and scientists in the food, biotechnology, analytical, agricultural, and advanced-materials industries.
