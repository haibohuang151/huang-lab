Engineering Food & Bioprocesses from Discovery to Deployment

We integrate food processing, biomanufacturing, separations, and techno‑economic analysis to translate laboratory discoveries into practical technologies.

Research Approach

We develop scalable approaches to transform food and agricultural resources into functional ingredients, biomaterials, and biochemicals for a more circular bioeconomy.
